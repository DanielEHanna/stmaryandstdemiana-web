Divine LiturgyWhat’s this?
The main service of the Coptic Orthodox Church: prayers, hymns and Bible readings, centred on Holy Communion.
CopticWhat’s this?
From the ancient word for “Egyptian”. The Coptic Church traces its roots to St. Mark, who brought Christianity to Alexandria in the first century.

Christian education classes for children and young people, grouped by age.
